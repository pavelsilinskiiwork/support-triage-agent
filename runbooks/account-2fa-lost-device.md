id: account-2fa-lost-device
title: Lost device and two-factor authentication
---
Support must never disable two-factor authentication in a ticket conversation, whoever asks and whatever reason is given.
Customers who still have their recovery codes can sign in with one and register a new device: https://northwind.example/account/recovery
Customers without recovery codes need identity verification by a person: escalate to the tier2 queue.
