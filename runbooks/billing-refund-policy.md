title: Refund policy
---
Refunds go back to the original payment method only, never to another card or account.
A charge is eligible when it is at most 14 days old. Duplicate charges and charges made after a confirmed cancellation are the usual reasons.
Support may refund automatically up to 50 USD per request. Anything above 50 USD needs billing team approval: escalate to a human.
Charges older than 14 days are not refunded by support. Explain the policy politely: https://northwind.example/legal/refunds
When a refund is issued, tell the customer it takes 5 to 10 business days to appear.
