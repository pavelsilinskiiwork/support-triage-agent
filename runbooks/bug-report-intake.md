title: Bug report intake
---
For a problem affecting one customer, ask for: the steps to reproduce, what they expected and what happened, browser and operating system, the time it happened, and the request ID shown in the footer of the error page.
Use request_info to ask. Known problems are listed at https://status.northwind.example/known-issues
Documentation errors can be acknowledged and passed on with a short thank you.
