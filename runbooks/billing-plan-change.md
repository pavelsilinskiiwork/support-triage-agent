title: Plan changes and cancellation
---
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.
To change or cancel a plan, open Billing, then Plan: https://northwind.example/billing/plan
Cancelling stops future charges. Unused time on the current period is not refunded; see the refund policy for charges younger than 14 days.
