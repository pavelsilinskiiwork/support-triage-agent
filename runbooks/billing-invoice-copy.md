title: Invoice copies and billing details
---
Invoices for every month are listed at https://northwind.example/billing/invoices and can be downloaded as PDF.
Only workspace admins can see the Billing page. VAT number, company name and billing address are edited under Billing settings.
Invoices cannot be re-issued under a different company name after the billing period closes; ask the customer to edit details before the next charge.
